01THE PREMISE

You could just go to the mountains.
Or you could make a plot out of it.

Anyone can book a hill station.
The hard part is finding the right people to be up there with.
That’s where Plot Twist comes in.

THE PLOT TWIST FORMULA
01
10/10 energy
Good stories. Big personalities. People who say yes before they know the plan.
02
One base, three nights
Bir, the whole time. Unpack once. Every day goes out from there and comes home to a fire.
03
Curated experiences
Café-hopping Bir, Barot’s waterfalls, a guided trek and two party nights — each day a different world.
04
20 people
Small enough to become a family. Big enough to never run out of stories.

You’ll fit right in if…

  • you’d run off a hill if someone counted to three.
  • you’d trade a lie-in for a sunrise. once, at least.
  • strangers become friends by the second bonfire.
  • you collect stories, not just places.
  • you pack a warm jacket and a sense of humour.
IF YOU NEED EVERYTHING TO GO TO PLAN…
you may have opened the wrong dossier.
akshat
A NOTE FROM THE GUY WHO STARTED THIS

Seven-plus countries, solo trips, group trips, too many late nights — and somewhere along the way I realised the best part of travelling was almost never the place. It was the people, the plans that changed, the stories that came out of nowhere. Plot Twist is my attempt to put more of those trips into the world.

— Akshat
