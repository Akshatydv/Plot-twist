## Three frames were laid in, inspected at full resolution, and pulled

This is the rule that matters, and it is the reason it exists. Every one of
these was chosen from a thumbnail, looked fine, and failed once opened at
1:1 — which is exactly how an `aiwa` sponsor logo and an `OPEN'ER FESTIVAL`
wristband got into finished teaser slides before they were caught.

- **`39257427`** — lasers and a ferris wheel, and **"H.I.V.E." spelled across
  the stage in letters you can read**. That is another event's stage branding
  on a page whose entire legal posture is that it is not affiliated with the
  event it names.
- **`258804`** — confetti and a huge crowd, and on inspection a **rock band on
  a thrust stage in an indoor arena**. Wrong genre for a chapter called ONE
  LAST DANCE, and the performers are identifiable.
- **`2263435`** — a spectacular symmetrical laser burst, with **legible Chinese
  text running across both side LED walls** and, again, an indoor roof.

Also rejected before download: `34766319` (clearly identifiable faces in the
front row plus a branded canopy) and `5156686` (sponsor banners on the right
of frame).

## The two rules these frames are chosen against

**1. No third-party branding, checked at FULL RESOLUTION.** Not from a
thumbnail, not from a contact sheet. See above for why.

**2. Nobody who could be mistaken for a named artist.** This page prints real
artist names elsewhere, and a recognisable performer under one reads as that
person — the implied-endorsement problem the whole campaign avoids, arriving
through the picture instead of the copy. `day-04` has no people in it at all;
`day-05` and `day-06` are shot from behind the crowd, facing the stage, with
no performer resolvable.

## A note on `day-03`

It is a resort, and it sits under copy that says "into the stay for the rest
of the week". A reader could take it for the actual hotel. It is not — no
property has been chosen, and naming or showing one would be the single
invented fact on an otherwise sourced page.

This is covered on the page rather than only here: `pass.disclaimer` states
that the photography is licensed stock and **does not show the actual venues,
stays or travellers on this trip**. If that disclaimer is ever edited, this
frame is one of the reasons it exists.

## The rule for anything added here

Record above: source, licence linked, photographer, and confirmation of both
checks **at full resolution**. If a frame contains anyone who could be read as
a named artist, it belongs on a chapter that names none, or nowhere.
